Frequently asked questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Which book should I start with?
If you have little or no electronics experience, start with Book 1 — Junior Tech Wizards In Basic Electronics. It lays the foundation you'll need for everything else. If you already understand basic electronics and want to jump into Arduino projects, you can start with Book 2. Book 3 is best enjoyed after Books 1 and 2.
Do I need any prior experience in electronics or coding?
Absolutely none. The series is designed from the ground up for complete beginners. Book 1 starts with the very basics — what electricity is, how it flows, and how simple components work. No prior knowledge of electronics or programming is required at any point in the series.
What age is this suitable for?
The series is written to be accessible to readers aged 10 and above. Younger children (8–10) can work through the books with a parent or teacher. Adults of any age will also find the content clear, practical, and engaging — it's been used by teens, hobbyists, and working professionals alike.
Do I need to buy an Arduino board or components to use these books?
Not immediately. Book 2 includes full Proteus simulation files so you can test and build every project virtually on your computer before buying any physical hardware. When you're ready to build for real, a basic Arduino Uno starter kit — widely available and affordable — is all you need.
Do I need to know how to code to use Book 3?
No coding experience is required at all. Book 3 teaches you how to use ChatGPT as your personal coding assistant — you'll learn how to describe what you want to build, prompt the AI to write the code, and understand enough to test, tweak, and debug it. The focus is on thinking like a builder, not a programmer.
Can teachers use these books in a classroom?
Yes — the series is an excellent classroom companion for electronics, ICT, and technology subjects. The step-by-step project structure, circuit diagrams, and simulation tools make it easy to integrate into lessons. Many educators use Book 1 as an introductory text and Book 2 as a practical project workbook.
Are the books available as eBooks or only in print?
Both formats are available. All three books can be purchased as Kindle eBooks for instant access on any device, or as paperbacks for a physical copy you can highlight, annotate, and keep on your workbench. Find both options on each book's Amazon page.
Is this series good for someone who wants to become an inventor?
It's one of the best starting points available. Inventors need three things: a solid understanding of how electronics work, the ability to build and test prototypes, and the tools to bring ideas to life quickly. This series gives you all three — electronics foundations, hands-on Arduino projects, and AI-assisted coding to accelerate your creative process.
